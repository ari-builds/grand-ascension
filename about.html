Our story

Better credit starts with understanding it.

I started Grand Ascension because a lot of people never get taught how credit works. Here is how I got here.

Founder & Credit Consultant

Jelinsky Alteus

"I graduated college with a Finance degree. One of the biggest things I struggled with was finding a career I actually wanted in the finance field. There are so many directions you can take, and I kept trying to figure out which one was mine."

So I did what made sense. I read books and I found a mentor to help me map out the path. The more I learned about credit, the clearer it got. Credit is a big part of financial literacy, and a lot of people either reject it or were never taught how to manage it for themselves.

To me, credit was one of the areas that made the most sense. I studied how it works, especially the laws that govern it, and got to a point where I understood the ins and outs of credit. That understanding is what led me to start this business.

The goal is simple. Help people understand their credit, then let that understanding open better opportunities. A better score means lower interest, better housing, better car options. That is what a brighter future looks like.
