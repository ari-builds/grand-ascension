What We Dispute

Every negative item is on the table.

We create personal dispute strategies for each type of mark on your report.

Late Payments

A single late payment can drop your score. We dispute them with supporting documentation.

Collections

Past-due entries and third-party collections. Don't let collections hold you back.

Repossessions

Disputed with a focus on verification gaps and documentation failures.

Closed Accounts

Closed accounts left behind can keep your score down. We challenge the records that remain.
