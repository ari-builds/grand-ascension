Privacy

Privacy Policy

What Grand Ascension collects, how it is used, and what we never share. No selling your data, ever.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to help you:

  • Your name, phone number, and email when you use the contact form or the chat widget.
  • The messages you send through the site chat, so we can respond and improve.
  • Files you attach to the contact form, such as screenshots you choose to send.
  • Booking details when you schedule on Calendly (handled by Calendly's own privacy policy too).
  • Standard analytics that a website host may record, like general location and pages visited. This site itself does not set tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your message and book your consultation.
  • To send the SMS reply after you text the CREDIT keyword.
  • To prepare the free consult and any credit analysis you request.
  • Never to sell your information. Ever.

What we share

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services needed to run the business, like our website host, Calendly for bookings, and a messaging provider for SMS replies. Where those providers touch your data, they are bound to keep it confidential. We never ask you to send sensitive data like Social Security numbers, card numbers, or bank account numbers through this website or by text.

Your choices

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold by emailing grandascensionllc@gmail.com. We answer these requests promptly.

Children

This site is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update this page and note the date below. Last updated: September 2026.
